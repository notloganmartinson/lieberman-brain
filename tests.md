When I talk about "high agency," I’m talking about the ability to move fast and control your destiny. Email allows for a direct, friction-less connection with your customers. You can test ideas, drive sales, and build community without asking for permission from an algorithm.

**The Bottom Line:**
AI is changing how we build products, but it hasn't changed human psychology. People still crave direct, curated connection. 

If you aren't building an owned email list, you’re just building on shifting sand. Stop renting your audience and start owning it.
